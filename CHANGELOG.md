# Changelog

Every change to `main` is built and published as a release automatically, so each release's notes list the exact changes. This file summarises the big milestones.

## 0.1 Preview (November 2026)

### Contacts and people
- Contacts tab rebuilt from scratch: eight display styles, A–Z strip, live updates, long-press menu
- Favourites rebuilt: grid or list, drag to reorder, same order in the favourites row
- New contact page: Call / Text / Video, apps card, socials, recent activity, email with copy and share
- Find-on linking for socials, with share-a-screenshot text recognition and the optional Link helper
- Ask for an email by text, with an offer to save it when they reply
- In-app contact editor
- App and @ icons under names

### Calls
- Recents rebuilt: grouped by day and person, five filters, swipe to call or text
- Keypad rebuilt: T9 name search, recent numbers, paste, spinning Omnio ring, hold 1 for voicemail
- New call screen with six styles, 15 answer designs, a live designer, and per-person and unknown-caller styles
- Call service and call notifications rebuilt
- Speed dial and blocked numbers screens, and call history export and import

### Messages
- Messages merged in as a tab, with reactions (seen and sent) and email chips
- The conversation title opens the contact page

### Everything else
- Unified Settings and About
- Omnio bottom sheets throughout
- Packages renamed to `com.omnioone`
