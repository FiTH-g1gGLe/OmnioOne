Omnio One brings your contacts, calls and messages together in one app.

Contacts
• 18 display styles, from simple lists to Orbit, Honeycomb and Polaroid
• Favourites you can reorder by holding and sliding
• Contact pages with chat and social app links, notes and birthdays
• Facebook friend matching and profile photo capture

Calls
• Design your own call screen: background, photo, the Omnio ring, 15 answer styles
• Smart keypad with contact search, speed dial and voicemail on hold 1
• Recents with filters, swipe actions and call history backup
• Blocking for numbers, patterns, unknown and hidden callers

Messages
• Conversation screen with photos, reactions, email chips and contact cards
• Undo send, SIM choice, verification codes copied for you
• Notifications with photos and quick reply
• Archive, 30-day recycle bin, blocked words, backup and restore

Everything stays on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
