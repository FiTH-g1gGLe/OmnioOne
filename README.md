## Why Omnio One?

Most phones split your people across three apps. Your contacts live in one, calls in another, texts in a third, and none of them knows that your friend is on WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger.

Omnio One brings them together. Open someone and everything about them is in one place: call, text or video, their chat apps and social profiles, your recent calls and texts with them, their email, birthday and notes. It's built to be quick, private and good-looking, and almost everything can be made your own.

| | |
|---|---|
| 📇 **One contact page for everything** | Call, text, video, chat apps, socials, recent activity, email: all on one screen |
| 🔗 **Link anyone's socials in seconds** | Share a profile or a screenshot to Omnio One and it works out who they are |
| 📲 **A call screen you design** | Six ready-made styles, 15 ways to answer, and a designer to build your own |
| ⚡ **Everything updates live** | Favourites, contacts, calls and texts change in front of you, no restarts |
| 🎨 **Your look, your layout** | Eight contact layouts, themes, and per-person call screens |
| 🔒 **Private by design** | No ads, no trackers, no account. Your data stays on your phone |

## Features

### 📇 Contacts
- **Eight display styles:** List, Compact, Detailed, Cards, Circles, Squares, Tiles and Speed dial, with density and first-name-only options
- **App icons under names** for the chat apps and socials each person uses, plus an **@** when you have their email
- **Favourites row** above the list, in the same order as the Favourites tab
- **A–Z strip** with a letter bubble and haptic ticks
- **Search** across names, nicknames, numbers, emails, company and job title
- **Long-press menu:** call (choose SIM), text, open, edit, favourite, share as a contact card, add to home screen, block, delete

### 👤 Contact page
- Big **Call · Text · Video** buttons, with the number and email under the name (copy and share in a tap)
- **Apps card:** WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Telegram, Signal, Viber and Google Meet are found automatically
- **Socials:** Instagram, Snapchat, Facebook, Messenger (message, call and video), TikTok, X, Threads, LinkedIn and Discord
- **Find-on linking:** search for them in the app, then share their profile link or a **screenshot** (read with on-device text recognition) to save it. The optional **Link helper** grabs the link for you
- **Ask for their email:** sends a friendly text, then offers to save the address when they reply
- **Recent activity:** your latest calls and texts with them, mixed and newest first
- **Built-in editor** with photo, labelled numbers and emails, birthday, work, socials, address, notes and which account to save to

### ⭐ Favourites
- Grid or list, with your choice of columns
- **Hold and drag** to put them in your own order; hold without moving for the menu
- Tap to call or to open, whichever you prefer

### 🕘 Recents
- Calls grouped by day (Today, Yesterday, weekdays, dates) and by person
- Filters: **All · Missed · Incoming · Outgoing · Unknown**
- **Swipe right to call, swipe left to text**
- Long-press for full call history, copy, block, or delete from Recents

### 🔢 Keypad
- Rounded keys with a **spinning Omnio ring** on every press
- **Type a name** on the number keys to find contacts (T9), with matches highlighted
- Your last eight numbers ready to tap, paste-a-number, add-to-contacts and text shortcuts
- Hold **0** for +, hold **1** for voicemail, hold **2–9** for speed dial, and press call with nothing typed to redial

### 📞 Calls
- **Six call-screen styles:** Classic, Poster, Minimal, Context, Pulse and **Orbit** (the Omnio ring spinning round their photo)
- **15 answer designs:** five each for buttons, swipe up/down and swipe left/right
- **Call screen designer** with a live preview: background, colours, photo shape and size, what to show, quick actions
- **Different styles for different people,** plus a separate style for unknown callers
- **Context while it rings:** their last text, birthday countdown, notes and chat apps
- In-call: mute, keypad, speaker/Bluetooth/headset, hold, add call and swap, plus reply-with-text and silence
- **Speed dial** for keys 1–9, and **blocked numbers** with automatic blocking of unknown or hidden callers

### 💬 Messages
- A full SMS/MMS messenger as a tab: pinned chats, filters, swipe to archive or mark read
- **Conversation screen:** bubbles with day dividers, photos, reactions shown on their message, email chips, contact cards, drafts
- **Sending:** photos, camera, files and contact cards, a SIM picker for dual-SIM phones, and **undo send**
- **Notifications** with the sender's photo, photo previews, quick reply and mark as read
- **New message screen** with recent people, search and group chats
- **Archive and recycle bin** (30 days to restore), blocked words, and **message backup and restore**

### ⚙️ Settings
- One settings page for everything: theme, contact display, language, call screen, contacts, calls, call history, messages, Link helper and default apps
- **Export and import your call history**

## Install

1. On your Android phone, **[download OmnioOne.apk](https://github.com/FiTH-g1gGLe/OmnioOne/releases/latest/download/OmnioOne.apk)** and open it.
2. Allow installing from your browser if Android asks.
3. If some permissions are greyed out, go to **Settings → Apps → Omnio One → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings**.
4. Open Omnio One and set it as your **Phone** and **SMS** app when asked.

> Omnio One is in **preview**. Every change is built and published automatically, so the download link above always gives you the newest version. Your contacts, calls and texts are stored by Android, not inside the app, so updating never touches them.

## Build it yourself

```bash
git clone https://github.com/FiTH-g1gGLe/OmnioOne.git
cd OmnioOne
./gradlew assembleFossDebug
```

The APK appears in `app/build/outputs/apk/foss/debug/`. You need JDK 17 and the Android SDK.

The project has two modules:

| Module | What it is |
|---|---|
| `app` | Contacts, phone, messages, call screen, settings |
| `commons` | Shared building blocks |
