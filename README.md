## 📖 What is Omnio One?

Most phones split your people across three apps. Contacts live in one, calls in another, texts in a third, and none of them knows that your friend is on WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger.

**Omnio One is one app for all of it.** It's your **contacts app**, your **phone app** and your **messages app**, built together from the ground up. Open anyone and everything about them is in one place: call, text or video, their chat apps and social profiles, your recent calls and texts, their email, birthday and notes.

It's quick, private and good-looking, and almost everything can be made your own.

## ✨ Features

### 📇 Contacts
- **18 display styles:** List, Compact, Detailed, Cards, Circles, Squares, Tiles, Speed dial, Bubbles, Timeline, Minimal, Business cards, Spotlight, Pills, Polaroid, **Orbit**, Honeycomb and Monogram
- **App icons under names** for the chat apps and socials each person uses, plus an **@** when you have their email
- **Favourites row** above the list: same order as the Favourites tab, **hold and drag to rearrange**, always the latest photo
- **A–Z strip** with a letter bubble and haptic ticks
- **Search** across names, nicknames, numbers, emails, company and job title (it clears itself when you change tab)
- **Long-press menu:** call (choose SIM), text, open, edit, favourite, share as a contact card, add to home screen, block, delete

### 👤 Contact page
- Big **Call · Text · Video** buttons, with number and email under the name (copy or share in a tap)
- **Chat apps found automatically:** WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Telegram, Signal, Viber and Google Meet
- **Socials:** Instagram, Snapchat, Facebook, Messenger (message, call and video), TikTok, X, Threads, LinkedIn and Discord
- **Find-on linking:** look someone up in the app, then share their profile link or a **screenshot** (read on your phone, nothing uploaded) to save it
- **Link helper** (optional): grabs the profile link and **profile photo** for you as you browse, behind a tidy Omnio loading card
- **Link Facebook friends:** import your friends list, match it to your contacts and link them one after another
- **Ask for their email:** sends a friendly text, then offers to save the address when they reply
- **Recent activity:** your latest calls and texts with them, newest first
- **Built-in editor:** photo, labelled numbers and emails, birthday, work, socials, address, notes and which account to save to

### ⭐ Favourites
- Grid or list, your choice of columns
- **Hold and drag** to set your own order; hold without moving for the menu
- Tap to **call** or to **open**, whichever you prefer

### 🕘 Recents
- Calls grouped by day (Today, Yesterday, weekdays, dates) and by person
- Filters: **All · Missed · Incoming · Outgoing · Unknown**
- **Swipe right to call, swipe left to text**
- Long-press for full call history, copy, block or delete

### 🔢 Keypad
- Rounded keys with the **spinning Omnio ring** on every press
- **Type a name** on the number keys (T9), with matches highlighted
- Your last eight numbers ready to tap, plus paste, add-to-contacts and text shortcuts
- Hold **0** for +, **1** for voicemail, **2–9** for speed dial; press call with nothing typed to redial

### 📞 Calls
- **Six call-screen styles:** Classic, Poster, Minimal, Context, Pulse and **Orbit** (the default: the Omnio ring spinning round their photo)
- **15 answer designs:** five each for buttons, swipe up/down and swipe left/right, with clear ✓ and ✕ icons
- **Call screen designer** with a live preview: background, colours, photo shape and size, what to show, quick actions
- **Different styles for different people,** plus one for unknown callers
- **Context while it rings:** their last text, birthday countdown, notes and chat apps
- In-call: mute, keypad, speaker/Bluetooth/headset, hold, add call and swap, reply with a text, silence
- **Speed dial** for keys 1–9 with search, and **blocked numbers** with automatic blocking of unknown or hidden callers
- **Export and import your call history**

### 💬 Messages
- A full **SMS and MMS messenger** as its own tab: pinned chats, filters (All, Unread, Unknown, Groups), swipe to archive or mark read
- **Conversation screen:** bubbles with day dividers, photos, contact cards, email chips, drafts and a friendly 💬 prompt
- **Reactions:** long-press for emojis first, see theirs on your message, and take yours back
- **Long-press menu with icons:** 📋 copy, ↪️ forward, 🔄 retry, 🗑️ delete
- **Sending:** photos, camera, files and contact cards from an emoji attach sheet, a SIM picker for dual-SIM phones, **undo send**, and the Omnio ring spinning on the send button
- **Verification codes:** copied for you automatically, tappable in the chat, and a **Copy** button right on the notification
- **Notifications** with the sender's photo, photo previews, quick reply and mark as read
- **New message screen** with recent people, search and group chats
- **Archive and recycle bin** (30 days to restore), **blocked words**, and **message backup and restore**

### ⚙️ Settings and look
- **One settings page** for appearance, contacts, call screen, default apps, calls, call history, messages and the Link helper
- **Open on** the tab you want (Contacts by default, or straight to the Keypad), and show or hide tabs
- Omnio's own dark theme, with a colour for each part: 🟠 contacts, 🔵 calls, 🟢 messages, 🟡 favourites, 🩷 socials
- **Omnio bottom sheets** everywhere instead of plain pop-ups

### 🔒 Private by design
- No ads, no trackers, no analytics, no account
- Screenshots are read **on your phone**: nothing is uploaded
- Your contacts, calls and texts stay in Android's own storage, so updating or reinstalling never touches them

## 📲 Install

1. On your Android phone, **[download OmnioOne.apk](https://github.com/FiTH-g1gGLe/OmnioOne/releases/latest/download/OmnioOne.apk)** and open it.
2. Allow installing from your browser if Android asks.
3. If some permissions are greyed out: **Settings → Apps → Omnio One → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings**.
4. Open Omnio One and set it as your **Phone** and **SMS** app when asked.

> [!NOTE]
> Omnio One is in **preview**. Every change is built and published automatically, so the link above always gives you the newest version. If something looks out of date after updating, uninstall and install fresh: your contacts, calls and texts are safe either way.

## 🛠️ Build it yourself

```bash
git clone https://github.com/FiTH-g1gGLe/OmnioOne.git
cd OmnioOne
./gradlew assembleFossDebug
```

The APK appears in `app/build/outputs/apk/foss/debug/`. You need **JDK 17** and the **Android SDK**.

| Folder | What's inside |
|---|---|
| `app/src/main/kotlin/com/omnioone/app/core` | Omnio's core: base screen, colours, dialogs, contacts engine |
| `…/activities` · `…/fragments` | Screens and tabs |
| `…/messaging` | The whole messenger: store, sending, receiving, notifications, backup |
| `…/helpers` · `…/views` · `…/adapters` | Call screen, sheets, search bar, contact styles |
| `.github/workflows/omnio-build.yml` | Builds, tests and publishes every change |
